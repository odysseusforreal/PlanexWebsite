Discover the Unknown

Awaken in the uncharted depths of the Planex galaxy, a massive, procedurally generated universe where every star is a sun, and every sun is orbited by unique, unexplored worlds. From toxic, luminescent fungal planets to desolate, frozen moons, the unknown awaits. You begin with nothing but a damaged exosuit and a stranded ship. Your first objective? Survive.

Gather resources, craft advanced technology, and rebuild your hyperdrive to take to the stars. Whether you choose to construct sprawling atmospheric bases, trade rare isotopes with other explorers, or brave the cosmic voids in search of ancient anomalies, your journey is entirely your own. Team up with friends, upgrade your fleet, and conquer the galaxy together.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Planex release?

Planex is currently in early development. Stay tuned to our Discord and YouTube channels for updates on alpha testing and official release dates.

How can I get alpha access?

Alpha access opportunities will be announced through our Discord server. Join our community to be the first to know!

What platforms will Planex be available on?

Planex is being developed exclusively for Roblox, which means you'll be able to play it on PC, mobile devices, and console.

Will there be multiplayer features?

Yes! Planex is designed with multiplayer experiences in mind. Team up with friends to explore vast planetary systems and create factions to dominate the galaxy!
